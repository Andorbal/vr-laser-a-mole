# Credits

## Game

Laser-a-Mole by Andrew Benz. Code, shaders, procedural art and audio are
released under the MIT license (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).

* **3D art:** every model, texture and effect is generated procedurally in
  code and shaders. There are no third-party art assets.
* **Sound effects and music:** synthesized from scratch by
  [`tools/generate_audio.py`](tools/generate_audio.py) (original work, MIT).
